One donation, four steps

  1. 1Donor posts surplus food.
  2. 2The nearest suitable NGOs are ranked and notified.
  3. 3A volunteer collects and delivers it.
  4. 4The NGO confirms what it received.

How it works

Donors

Post what is left over, see an AI estimate of typical surplus, and watch the donation move from pickup to delivery.

NGOs

Receive ranked offers for the food you can actually use, accept or reject with a reason, and confirm what arrived.

Volunteers

Set your availability, pick up nearby tasks, and update the delivery as you go.

Built to be trustworthy

Surplus and demand estimates

Machine-learning estimates shown with the model name, error and a clear "synthetic data" label.

Smart NGO matching

Distance, capacity, category, expiry and need are scored. If the AI is down, rule-based ranking takes over.

Verified organisations

Donors and NGOs are verified by an admin before they can take part.

Secure by design

Role-based access, short-lived sessions and an audit trail for important actions.
